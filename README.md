Employee Attrition & HR Analytics Dashboard

📊 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition patterns across a 1,470-employee organization. The dashboard identifies who is leaving, why, and where HR should focus retention efforts — using department filters, gender breakdowns, and role-level attrition analysis.

🎯 Objective

To help HR and management move from reactive to proactive workforce planning by visualizing attrition trends across demographics, compensation bands, tenure, and job roles — instead of relying on gut-feel assumptions about turnover.

🧰 Tools & Tech
Power BI Desktop — dashboard design, DAX measures, data modeling
Power Query — data cleaning and transformation
DAX — calculated KPIs (Attrition Rate, Avg Salary, Avg Tenure, etc.)

📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)
Total Employees: 1,470
Employees Left: 237
Attrition Rate: 16.1%
Average Age: 37
Average Salary: 6.5K
Average Years of Service: 7

🔍 Dashboard Sections
Attrition by Education — breakdown by education field (Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, Technical Degree, Other)
Attrition by Age Group — 5 age bands from 18 to 55+
Attrition by Salary Band — from under 5K to 15K+
Years of Service Trend — attrition pattern across tenure length
Job Satisfaction Matrix — satisfaction rating (1-4) by job role
Attrition by Job Role — top affected roles
Gender Split — Male vs. Female attrition counts
Department Filter — dynamic slicer for department-level drill-down

💡 Key Insights
Attrition is concentrated among employees aged 26-35, earning under 5K, with a Life Sciences background.
Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives are the most affected roles by volume.
Most attrition occurs very early in tenure (within the first year of service).
Male employees (140) leave at nearly double the rate of female employees (79).
